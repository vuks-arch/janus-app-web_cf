Our Story

Ember & Oak began in a tiny corner shop with one roaster and a stubborn belief that great coffee should feel like home. Today we still roast every bean in-house, in small batches, and pull each shot by hand.

Whether you're here for a quick espresso or a slow afternoon with a book, there's always a warm seat and a fresh cup waiting.
